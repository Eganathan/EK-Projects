A comprehensive all-in-one platform designed to centralize every aspect of personal life management. Instead of juggling multiple separate applications for different needs, Persona provides a unified dashboard where you can login and instantly see what's happening in your life across all dimensions.

**Note**: This is a very ambitious project currently in the vision stage, with development planned for the future.

## Problem Statement

Personal life management is currently fragmented across dozens of different apps and platforms. Managing tasks in one app, notes in another, contacts elsewhere, mood tracking in yet another tool, and memories scattered across various platforms creates unnecessary complexity and maintenance overhead. This fragmentation makes it difficult to get a holistic view of your life and progress.

## Vision

One login, one dashboard, complete life overview. Persona aims to be the single source of truth for everything an individual wants to store, track, and manage in their personal life.

## Core Features

### Content Management System (CMS)
- Personal content creation and organization
- Custom templates for different types of entries
- Rich media support for all content types

### Task & Project Management
- Integrated task tracking across all life areas
- Project management with timelines and milestones
- Goal setting and progress tracking

### Contact Management
- Comprehensive phone number and contact database
- Relationship tracking and interaction history
- Contact context and personal notes

### Personal Documentation
- Universal note-taking system
- Knowledge base for personal reference
- Document storage and organization

## Life Tracking Features

### Memory Management
- Digital memory preservation and organization
- Timeline view of important life events

### Mood & Emotional Tracking

- **Anger Notes**: Track and analyze anger triggers and responses

### Custom Data Storage
- Flexible data entry for any personal information
- Custom categories and tags for organization
