# LinkX - Organized Link Management Platform

A powerful link management platform designed to solve the common problem of losing incredible playlists and resources shared on social media platforms. Instead of manually searching through notes or keep apps after links vanish from WhatsApp stories or other temporary sharing platforms, LinkX provides a permanent, organized solution.

## Problem Statement

The frustration of discovering amazing playlists, articles, and resources online, sharing them temporarily through social media stories, only to lose them after 24 hours and having to manually hunt through various note-taking apps to find them again.

## Key Features

- **Nested Folder Organization**: Create multiple levels of folders to categorize links hierarchically
- **Privacy Controls**: Choose which links to share publicly and which to keep private
- **Folder Privacy Settings**: Control visibility at the folder level
- **Community Features**: Discover and share curated link collections with others
- **Persistent Storage**: Never lose your valuable links again
- **Organized Structure**: Say goodbye to chaotic bookmark lists

## Technologies Used

- **Node.js**: Backend runtime for server-side logic
- **InstantDB**: Real-time database for seamless data synchronization
- **Modern Web Standards**: Built with performance and user experience in mind

## Features in Detail

### Organization System
- Create unlimited nested folders for perfect categorization
- Drag and drop interface for easy link management
- Bulk operations for efficient organization
- Search and filter capabilities across all stored links

### Privacy & Sharing
- Individual link privacy settings
- Folder-level privacy controls
- Share curated collections with friends or the community
- Keep personal links completely private

### Community Features
- Discover popular link collections from other users
- Follow users with great curation taste
- Community recommendations based on your interests
- Collaborative folder sharing options

This platform transforms the way you manage and share digital resources, ensuring that valuable content is never lost in the 24-hour cycle of social media stories.
