# Tazk - Personal Task Management Tool

A powerful personal task management platform designed to organize and track tasks across all areas of life. From work assignments to home chores, personal projects to event planning, Tazk provides the structure and features needed to stay organized and productive.

## Problem Statement

Task management is extremely essential for anyone working - whether it's home tasks, work tasks, personal projects, or planning events for speeches. Without proper organization, important tasks get forgotten, deadlines are missed, and productivity suffers.

## Key Features

- **Group Separations**: Organize tasks into distinct groups (work, home, personal projects, etc.)
- **Task Timers**: Built-in timers for each task to track time spent and maintain focus
- **Detailed Notes**: Add comprehensive notes to each task for context and instructions
- **Personal Organization**: Tailored for individual productivity and task tracking
- **Multi-Context Support**: Handle everything from daily chores to complex project planning

## Planned Features

- **Community Features**: Share task templates and productivity strategies with others
- **Collaboration Tools**: Work on shared tasks and projects with team members
- **Advanced Analytics**: Track productivity patterns and time management insights

## Use Cases

### Work Tasks
- Project deadlines and milestones
- Meeting preparations and follow-ups
- Daily work assignments and priorities

### Home Management
- Household chores and maintenance
- Family event planning and coordination
- Personal errands and appointments

### Personal Projects
- Side project development and tracking
- Learning goals and skill development
- Creative projects and hobbies

### Event Planning
- Speech preparation and practice sessions
- Event coordination and logistics
- Timeline management for special occasions

## Why Tazk is Essential

Task management is fundamental to productivity and success in any area of life. Tazk provides the structure needed to:
- Never forget important tasks or deadlines
- Track time spent on different activities
- Maintain context with detailed task notes
- Organize tasks by life areas for better focus
- Plan and execute complex events and projects

This tool has become extremely useful for maintaining organization across all aspects of personal and professional life, ensuring nothing falls through the cracks.
